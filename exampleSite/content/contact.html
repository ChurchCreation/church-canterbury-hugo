Contact

The office is open Tuesday to Friday, 9.30 to 12.30, and the phone is answered by a person rather than a menu.

The parish office is open Tuesday to Friday mornings and the phone is answered by a person. For a death, ring any time — it diverts out of hours.

Getting here

I

By car

Fore Street car park, BA22 9TB, three minutes' walk and free on Sundays. Two accessible spaces at the lych gate on Church Green itself.

Directions
II

By bus

The 68 and the 81 stop on Fore Street. Sunday services are sparse — check before relying on the last one home.

III

Access

Step-free through the south porch, accessible WC in the north aisle, hearing loop throughout, large-print books at the back. The tower and gallery are not accessible. Assistance dogs always welcome.
